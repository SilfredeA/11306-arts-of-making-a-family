# 11,306 Arts of Making a Family

A book of 11,306 leaves. The cover is an empty black plate, lettered in gold. Inside, evil is asked to meet its own face, and a family is made by sight, account, mercy, and ordinary work.

Each leaf is its own edition: a figured painting with the hands and both legs kept whole, and a fragment of the old books held in the margin. The quarry keeps those folios unpainted.

Open the book, then the eleven doors.
